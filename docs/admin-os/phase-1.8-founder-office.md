# AURELIX ADMIN OS

# PHASE 1.8 — Founder Office & Executive Governance

## Objective

Design the Founder Office as the highest governance authority inside Aurelix Admin OS. The Founder Office is responsible for company vision, governance, critical approvals, executive management, emergency control, and strategic decision-making.

---

# Founder Office Philosophy

Founder Office is NOT another department. It is the highest executive authority of the company. Every critical company decision ultimately belongs to the Founder Office. Founder Office controls governance, not day-to-day departmental work.

---

# Founder Office Members

**Launch Version**
- Founder
- Co-Founder(s)

**Future**
- CEO
- Board Members (Optional)
- Executive Advisors (Optional)
- Observers (Optional)

---

# Founder Office Responsibilities

- Company Vision
- Company Strategy
- Company Governance
- Department Governance
- Executive Appointments
- Critical Policy Decisions
- Emergency Decisions
- Company Expansion
- Acquisition Approval
- Investment Approval
- Major Budget Approval
- Global Platform Decisions

---

# Founder Office Authority

Founder Office can:
- Create Department
- Archive Department
- Merge Departments
- Split Departments
- Appoint Department Head
- Remove Department Head
- Approve Critical Hiring
- Approve Critical Promotions
- Approve Critical Terminations
- Approve Company Policies
- Approve Global Settings
- Emergency Override
- Global Announcement
- Platform Emergency Actions
- Company Configuration

---

# Founder Office Cannot

- Delete Audit Logs
- Modify Historical Records
- Delete Company History
- Bypass Audit Logging

Every Founder action must be recorded.

---

# Founder Dashboard

Founder Office Dashboard includes:
- Company Health
- Department Health
- Revenue Overview
- Employee Overview
- Critical Alerts
- Security Incidents
- Pending Executive Approvals
- Weekly Reports
- Monthly Reports
- Department KPIs
- Platform Status
- Growth Metrics
- Emergency Actions
- System Notifications
- Company Timeline

---

# Executive Inbox

Founder Office receives only executive-level items.

Examples:
- Critical HR Requests
- Department Creation
- Department Head Appointment
- Critical Policy Approval
- Emergency Security Alerts
- Large Budget Requests
- Major Incident Reports
- Legal Notices
- Company Risks

Everything else remains within departments.

---

# Executive Approval System

Every executive approval includes:
```
Request → Business Justification → Impact Analysis → Risk Analysis →
Executive Review → Approval / Rejection → Audit Log
```

---

# Executive Decision Log

Every Founder Office decision automatically creates:
- Decision ID
- Decision Title
- Reason
- Alternatives Considered
- Approved By
- Approval Date
- Affected Departments
- Implementation Status
- Version
- Audit Reference

Decision history can never be deleted.

---

# Executive Calendar

Founder Office calendar includes:
- Board Meetings
- Department Reviews
- Quarterly Reviews
- Annual Planning
- Product Reviews
- Hiring Reviews
- Budget Reviews
- Audit Reviews
- Compliance Reviews
- Incident Reviews

---

# Executive Reports

Founder Office automatically receives:
- Weekly Department Reports
- Monthly Department Reports
- Quarterly Reviews
- Annual Reviews
- Security Reports
- Finance Reports
- Compliance Reports
- Incident Reports
- Growth & Strategy Reports

Reports are read-only for archival integrity.

---

# Emergency Powers

Founder Office may invoke emergency actions:
- Freeze Department
- Freeze Employee
- Freeze Platform Feature
- Force Logout All Sessions
- Emergency Policy Override
- Emergency Security Lockdown
- Emergency Broadcast

Every emergency action is:
- Logged immutably
- Notified to Security & Legal
- Reviewed post-incident

---

# Dual Founder Control

For extreme actions (e.g., platform-wide shutdown, mass permission revocation, founder-role changes), require **two Founder Office approvals** where policy dictates.

---

# Executive Notifications

Founder Office receives priority notifications for:
- Critical Incidents
- High-Risk Approvals
- Legal Alerts
- Security Breaches
- Financial Anomalies
- Executive Requests
- Board-Level Communications

---

# Founder Office Audit

Every Founder Office event is logged:
- Decision Made
- Department Created / Archived
- Head Appointed / Removed
- Policy Approved
- Emergency Action Executed
- Override Used
- Report Reviewed

Audit logs are immutable and independently reviewable.

---

# Future Expansion

Architecture must support:
- Board of Directors module
- External Advisors
- Investor Relations
- Multiple Founder Tiers
- Regional Executive Offices

without redesigning the executive governance engine.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Phase 1.8 is complete when:
- Founder Office philosophy is documented.
- Members, responsibilities, and authority are defined.
- Boundaries (what Founder cannot do) are documented.
- Founder dashboard is specified.
- Executive inbox, approvals, and decision log are defined.
- Executive calendar and reports are specified.
- Emergency powers and dual-control rules are documented.
- Audit requirements are finalized.

No implementation was performed in this phase.
