# AURELIX ADMIN OS

# PHASE 1.6 — Department Structure & Department Architecture

## Objective

Design the complete department architecture of Aurelix Admin OS. Every department must have clearly defined responsibilities, ownership, authority boundaries, collaboration rules, reporting structure, and future scalability.

---

# Department Philosophy

Every department is an independent operational unit. Each department owns its own responsibilities. Departments collaborate with each other but cannot interfere with another department's authority unless permitted through approved workflows.

Every department has:
- Department Head
- Deputy Department Head
- Team Leads
- Specialists
- Associates
- Interns (optional)

---

# Department Ownership

Every department must define:
- Department Name
- Department Mission
- Department Description
- Department Owner
- Deputy Head
- Reporting Structure
- Primary Responsibilities
- Related Departments
- KPIs
- Department Status
- Department Creation Date
- Department Version
- Department Documentation

---

# Department Status

Departments support:
- Active
- Under Setup
- Maintenance
- Suspended
- Archived

Only Founder Office may change department status.

---

# Department Relationships

Departments may collaborate but remain independent.

Example:
- Trust & Safety ↔ Verification ↔ ADOS ↔ Legal
- Engineering ↔ Security ↔ Analytics ↔ Support
- Finance ↔ HR ↔ Founder Office
- Support ↔ Verification ↔ Trust & Safety ↔ Organizations

Relationship mapping must remain configurable.

---

# Department Responsibilities

Each department owns only its assigned responsibilities. No department may take ownership of another department's responsibilities without approval.

Example — Verification

Owns:
- Blue Tick
- Organization Verification
- Identity Verification

Cannot:
- Suspend Employees
- Release Payments
- Approve Legal Requests

# Department Dashboard

Every department receives its own dashboard.

Dashboard shows:
- Pending Tasks
- Department KPIs
- Weekly Report
- Monthly Report
- Announcements
- Team Activity
- Performance
- Risks
- Quick Actions
- Department Calendar

The dashboard is customized according to department responsibilities.

---

# Department Members

Every member belongs to only one primary department.

Future support:
- Temporary Project Assignments
- Cross Department Projects
- Acting Assignments

Employee Passport automatically records department history.

---

# Department Capacity

Every department tracks:
- Current Employees
- Maximum Capacity
- Vacancies
- Hiring Requests
- Workload
- Growth Trend

Founder Office can monitor department health.

---

# Department Documents

Every department has its own document repository.

Examples:
- Policies
- SOPs
- Training Material
- Guidelines
- Templates
- Internal Forms

Documents remain version controlled.

---

# Department Knowledge Center

Every department maintains its own knowledge base.

Examples:
- Support — Support Guides
- Engineering — Coding Standards
- Finance — Financial Procedures
- Legal — Compliance Documents

Knowledge ownership belongs to the department.

---

# Department Reports

Mandatory Reports:
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Annual

Reports include:
- Completed Work
- Pending Work
- KPIs
- Problems
- Improvement Plans
- Hiring Needs
- Automation Suggestions

Founder Office has visibility across all reports.

---

# Department KPIs

Every department defines measurable KPIs.

Examples:
- Support — Average Response Time
- Verification — Verification Accuracy
- Engineering — Deployment Success Rate
- Finance — Payment Processing Time
- HR — Hiring Completion Rate

KPIs remain configurable.

---

# Cross Department Collaboration

Departments may request assistance from other departments.

Workflow:
```
Request → Review → Approval → Assignment → Completion → Audit Log
```

Every collaboration request receives a tracking ID.

---

# Department Creation

Only Founder Office may:
- Create Department
- Rename Department
- Merge Departments
- Split Departments
- Archive Departments

All actions require audit logs.

---

# Department Deletion

Departments cannot be permanently deleted. Only archived. Historical data must always remain available.

---

# Department Audit Logs

Every department records:
- Employee Joined
- Employee Left
- Head Changed
- Deputy Changed
- Policy Updated
- KPI Changed
- Department Created
- Department Archived
- Report Submitted
- Knowledge Updated

Audit logs cannot be modified.

---

# Notifications

Department notifications include:
- New Employee

- Project Assignment
- Knowledge Update

# Future Expansion

Architecture must support:
- Regional Departments

- Subsidiaries

without redesign.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Phase 1.6 is complete when:
- All launch departments are defined.
- Department ownership is documented.
- Department dashboards are defined.
- Department relationships are documented.
- Reporting structure is completed.
- Department KPIs are configurable.
- Collaboration workflows are documented.
- Department lifecycle is finalized.
- Audit requirements are complete.

No department-specific business logic was implemented during this phase.
