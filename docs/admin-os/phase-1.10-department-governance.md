# AURELIX ADMIN OS

# PHASE 1.10 — Department Governance & Operational Management

## Objective

Design a standardized governance framework for every department inside Aurelix Admin OS. This phase defines how departments operate, how leadership functions, how responsibilities are assigned, how work is monitored, and how departments remain efficient as the company grows.

---

# Department Governance Philosophy

Every department is independently managed but follows the same company governance framework. The objective is to create consistency across every department while allowing flexibility according to departmental responsibilities.

- No department should depend on one individual.
- Every department must be capable of operating continuously.

---

# Department Leadership

```
Department Head
   ↓
Deputy Department Head
   ↓
Team Leads
   ↓
Senior Specialists
   ↓
Specialists
   ↓
Associates
   ↓
Interns
```

---

# Department Head Responsibilities

- Department Quality
- Department Strategy
- Skill Verification
- Promotion Recommendations
- Team Structure
- Department KPIs
- Department Reports
- Knowledge Management
- Training Programs
- Department Planning
- Hiring Recommendations
- Department Performance Reviews
- Department Risk Management

---

# Deputy Department Head Responsibilities

- Assist Department Head
- Handle delegated approvals
- Manage department during Head absence
- Review escalated work
- Coordinate Team Leads
- Support department planning
- Department continuity management

Cannot permanently replace Department Head without Founder Office approval.

---

# Team Lead Responsibilities

- Assign Work
- Manage Team
- Daily Monitoring
- Review Employee Performance
- Approve Operational Tasks
- Mentor Employees
- Weekly Team Report
- Escalate Issues
- Training Support
- Quality Review

---

# Senior Specialist Responsibilities

- Handle difficult cases
- Technical mentoring
- Review complex work
- Knowledge contribution
- Internal training
- Support Team Leads
- Assist audits

---

# Specialist Responsibilities

- Perform operational work
- Handle assigned cases
- Maintain quality
- Participate in training
- Follow SOPs

---

# Associate Responsibilities

- Handle basic operations
- Complete assigned work
- Learn department processes
- Participate in onboarding programs

---

# Intern Responsibilities

- Training
- Shadowing
- Learning
- Restricted operational access
- No critical approvals

---

# Department Structure Rules

Every department must define:
- Department Mission
- Department Vision
- Department Goals
- Department KPIs
- Reporting Structure
- Documentation
- Training Plan
- Knowledge Base
- Approval Matrix
- Escalation Matrix
- Backup Plan

---

# Team Formation

Department Head creates teams based on:
- Workload
- Projects
- Specialization
- Employee Skills
- Business Needs

Every employee belongs to:
- Primary Team
- Primary Department

Future support:
- Temporary Project Teams
- Cross-functional Teams

---

# Team Lead Assignment

Department Head appoints Team Leads.

Selection based on:
- Performance
- Leadership
- Technical Skills
- Audit Score
- Training
- Department Requirements

All appointments require audit logs.

---

# Skill Verification

```
Employee submits skill → Pending Verification → Department Review →
Technical Evaluation → Department Head Approval → Skill Verified →
Employee Passport Updated
```

HR cannot verify technical skills.

---

# Promotion Recommendation

```
Performance Review → Team Lead Recommendation → Department Head Review →
HR Validation → Founder Office (higher levels) → Promotion →
Employee Passport Updated
```

---

# Department KPIs

Each department defines KPIs.

Examples:
- Support — Average Resolution Time
- Verification — Accuracy
- Engineering — Deployment Success
- HR — Hiring Completion
- Finance — Payment Accuracy

KPIs remain configurable.

---

---

# Department Continuity

Every department must have:
- Deputy Head
- Backup Team Lead
- Knowledge Documentation
- Cross Training
- Critical Process Documentation

No Single Point of Failure.

---

# Cross Department Collaboration

Departments collaborate using approved requests.

Workflow:
```
Request → Receiving Department Review → Approval →
Assignment → Completion → Audit Log
```

All requests receive tracking IDs.

---

# Department Charter

Every department has a permanent charter.

Includes:
- Mission
- Responsibilities
- Authority
- KPIs
- Approval Powers
- Escalation Path
- Documentation Standards
- Training Standards
- Department Policies

Only Founder Office can approve or modify Department Charters. Version history is mandatory.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Phase 1.10 is complete when:
- Department governance is fully documented.
- Leadership responsibilities are finalized.
- Team structure is documented.
- Skill verification workflow is finalized.
- Promotion recommendation process is defined.
- Department reporting framework is complete.
- Department continuity rules are documented.
- Department Charter is finalized.
- Audit requirements are completed.

No frontend, backend or database implementation was performed during this phase.
